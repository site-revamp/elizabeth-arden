Elizabeth Arden Homepage Redesign
1. Website Overview
Elizabeth Arden is a premium beauty brand offering skincare, cosmetics, fragrances, bath and body products, and beauty gift sets. Founded in 1910, the brand combines its heritage in luxury beauty with signature skincare innovations.

The redesigned homepage focuses on a more structured shopping experience, clearer promotional priorities and stronger integration of the brand's heritage and signature products.
2. Main Improvements
The redesign addresses three key problems identified during the website review.
1. Improved Product Discovery
- Introduced more visually intuitive product category entry points.
- Prioritised key shopping categories and collections.
- Simplified the product discovery experience to make navigating the extensive catalogue easier.
2. Clearer Promotional Hierarchy
- Established a more deliberate structure for promotional content.
- Prioritised the main campaign instead of giving multiple offers equal visual attention.
- Improved the presentation of signature products, campaigns and shopping pathways.
3. Stronger Brand Heritage and Product Storytelling
- Integrated Elizabeth Arden's heritage into the homepage experience.
- Gave greater prominence to signature products, including Eight Hour® Cream and Ceramide Capsules.
- Strengthened the presentation of the brand's history and product innovations through editorial storytelling and collection-focused sections.
3. Design Improvements
- Visual presentation: Strengthened the premium beauty aesthetic through more deliberate product imagery and editorial presentation.
- Layout: Created a clearer content hierarchy across promotional, product discovery and brand storytelling sections.
- Navigation: Improved the visibility and organisation of primary shopping categories.
- User experience: Established more direct pathways to product categories, signature collections and promotional campaigns.
- Brand consistency: Connected Elizabeth Arden's heritage, signature products and contemporary beauty positioning within a cohesive homepage experience.
- Overall appearance: Created a more structured and refined retail presentation that balances shopping priorities with brand storytelling.
